# Survival Guide and Salvamont Rules in Romania

## 1.Emergency Numbers
- **Salvamont :** (+40)725826668
- **Emergency Number:** 112 (prefered for every emergency)
## 2.Equipmanet
### Summer Season:
- Hiking boots with good traction
- Backpack suited for the duration
- Waterproof jacket
- Layered clothing(avoid cotton and choose synthetic materials)
- First aid kit
- Headlamp
- Water (around 2 liters) and energy snacks
- Emergency foil

### Winter Season(aditional):
- crampons and ice axe
- climbing helmet
- avalnche rescue kit
- gaiters and insulated gloves

## 3.Romanian Trail Markings:
- **Stripe/Band(Red,Yellow,Blue):** Main ridge trails or major trunk routes
- **Cross (Red, Yellow, Blue):** Connecting trails or valley junctions
- **Triangle (Red, Yellow, Blue):** Secondary trails, valleys, or paths leading toward peaks
- **Dot / Circle (Red, Yellow, Blue):** Loop (circuit) trails or short-distance walks

## 4. Essential Safety Rules
1. **Weather Check:** Never head out on the trail without prior checking of the weather forecast and avalanche danger (in winter).
2. **Time Management:** Start early in the morning to avoid being caught on the mountain after dark.
3. **Stay on the Trail:** Do not wander off the marked paths.
4. **Wildlife Encounters (Bears):** Stay calm, speak in a steady tone, do not run, and slowly back away while facing the animal.
