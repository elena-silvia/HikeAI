# Survival Guide and Salvamont Rules in Romania

## 1. Emergency Numbers
- **Salvamont Dispatch:** 0-SALVAMONT / (+40)725826668
- **Emergency Number:** 112 (preferred for any mountain emergency)

## 2. Equipment
### Summer Season:
- Hiking boots with good traction (Vibram sole recommended)
- Backpack suited for the duration
- Waterproof and windproof jacket
- Layered clothing (avoid cotton, choose synthetic / merino wool)
- First aid kit
- Headlamp with fresh batteries
- Water (around 2 liters per person) and energy snacks
- Emergency foil / survival blanket

### Winter Season (additional):
- Crampons and ice axe
- Climbing helmet
- Avalanche rescue kit (transceiver, probe, shovel)
- Gaiters and insulated gloves

## 3. Romanian Trail Markings:
- **Stripe / Band (Red, Yellow, Blue):** Main ridge trails or major trunk routes
- **Cross (Red, Yellow, Blue):** Connecting trails or valley junctions
- **Triangle (Red, Yellow, Blue):** Secondary trails, valleys, or paths leading toward peaks
- **Dot / Circle (Red, Yellow, Blue):** Loop (circuit) trails or short-distance walks

## 4. Essential Safety Rules
1. **Weather Check:** Never head out on the trail without checking the mountain weather forecast and avalanche risk (in winter).
2. **Time Management:** Start early in the morning to avoid being caught on the mountain after dark.
3. **Stay on the Trail:** Do not wander off marked paths.
4. **Wildlife Encounters (Bears):** Stay calm, speak in a steady loud tone, do not run, and slowly back away while facing the animal.
